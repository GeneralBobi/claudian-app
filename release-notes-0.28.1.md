# Claudian 0.28.1

- Yüzük can take notes from a video call: choose **Meeting: computer audio + microphone** (or computer audio only) under Sound source. What the speakers play (Zoom, Meet, a video) is transcribed on this computer alongside your microphone. Use headphones so each sentence arrives once, and tell the participants you are taking notes.
- Notes from a meeting are titled "Toplantı" and say they came from computer audio.
- The Start listening and Stop buttons carry the same microphone and stop icons as the phone app.
