# Claudian 1.6.2

Phone app Yüzük 0.11.2 ships with this release.

- **Deleting syncs too.** A note you delete or move to the Recycle Bin on your computer no longer comes back from the phone. The computer remembers what disappeared since the last sync, and the phone deletes its copy instead of sending it back.
- **The computer wins a first-time conflict.** When a note has never been synced from this vault and the two sides differ, the computer's version stays and the phone's version is kept in the computer's sync backup. Before, the phone's older copy could win because a freshly downloaded file looks new.
- Older phone versions can no longer sync; they are asked to update.
