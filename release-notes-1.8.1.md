# Claudian 1.8.1

- **Yüzük notes contain only the note.** The closing "Yüzük: the conversation was transcribed …" line is gone, as is the date, duration and sentence-count line under the title. Date and source stay in the note's properties; duration, sentence count and the writer are shown in Yüzük.
- Yüzük for Android 0.11.5: a recording no longer stops silently. If the recorder fails or its file stops growing, recording continues in a new part and the parts are joined when you stop. When the saved audio is shorter than the time on the clock, the recording shows both (for example 34/79 min).
- Yüzük for Android 0.11.4: a note written on the computer is always written to the phone's Obsidian as well, and sync runs on its own after a note and when the app opens.
