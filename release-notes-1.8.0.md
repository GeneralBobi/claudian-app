# Claudian 1.8.0

- **One address for every AI app: https://mcp.claudian.app/mcp.** Add it to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity. The browser shows an 8-character code; type it into Claudian → Connections → Shared address, check who is asking, and approve. The AI app connects to your own computer.
- **Nothing is stored on the way.** The shared address keeps only the apps that registered, a pairing request for five minutes, and a one-way hash of each token pointing to your device. Tokens are created and checked only by Claudian on your computer.
- **Your existing connections stay as they are.** Device addresses on relay.claudian.app keep working, and you still choose in Claudian which AI apps may connect and whether they may write.
- The GPU library for NVIDIA cards is now downloaded from its publisher (pinned), so it is not part of every release.
